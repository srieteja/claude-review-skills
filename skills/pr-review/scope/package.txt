## Your slice

You are reviewing **one slice of a larger PR**. The diff at __DIFF__ is a subset. Other
slices are being reviewed in parallel by other reviewers; the cross-package export surface
is being reviewed separately. Do not try to cover the whole PR.

The full list of changed files, with line counts, is at __FILES__. You can read any file in
the worktree, and should whenever your slice's code depends on it — a caller in another
slice is exactly the context that tells you whether a change here is a defect.

**Test files are left out of your diff**; a separate pass reviews them after you. They are
still in the worktree. Read them: a test is the clearest statement of what the author
believed the code guarantees.

Report only defects whose mechanism runs through code **in this slice**. A bug you notice in
another slice's diff is not yours — it will be covered there, and reporting it produces a
duplicate that the verifier will refute on scope.
